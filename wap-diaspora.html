African expertise abroad

African academics and scientists don't stop being part of the record when they move. This tracker follows African-born researchers, academics, and scientists who are now based in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Germany, or France — through their residence, workplace, or employer, wherever Wikidata records it — and checks whether their Wikipedia coverage keeps up with where they actually are.

Same mission as the Content & Gender Gap Tracker, aimed at a different gap: the story of African expertise doesn't stop at the border, and neither should the record of it.

Diaspora overview

African academics & scientists per receiving country, Wikipedia coverage, and the gender split.
Who counts: a person's citizenship (P27) must be one of the 54 African countries — no further nationality narrowing; every receiving country checks all 54. Their occupation must also match scientist / researcher / academic / university teacher or a subclass of one, and Wikidata must record a tie to the receiving country — residence (P551), work location (P937), or an employer (P108) based there. Someone with none of those three signals recorded won't show up here even if they are, in fact, living abroad — this reflects Wikidata's coverage, not the full reality.

Language coverage: same as the main tracker — English, French, Arabic, and Portuguese are checked for every country, plus each receiving country's own language on top (German, for Germany).

Freshness: this page reads precomputed numbers from diaspora-data.json instead of querying Wikidata live in your browser — the heavy queries used to time out constantly against the shared public endpoint, so they now run offline on a schedule instead. See the timestamp above for how current the numbers are.

Data: Wikidata Query Service, precomputed offline on a schedule (not queried live in your browser) and served as a static file. Nationality via P27 against the same 54 African countries as the main tracker, no further narrowing — every receiving country checks all 54; occupation match: scientist, academic/university teacher, or researcher (P106), including any subclass of one; diaspora residency via P551 (residence), P937 (work location), or P108→P17 (employer's country); gender via P21.
